The house

Made with looms, not shortcuts.

Matina Collection By Kls works with weavers in Varanasi, Kanchipuram and Jaipur, and with embroiderers who still sit with a frame on their lap. Every piece is finished in small batches — which is why a Banarasi from us feels like an heirloom, not a trend.
